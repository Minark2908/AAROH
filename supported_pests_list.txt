AAROH Project - Supported Pests for Detection
=============================================

This list represents the 40 specific pest categories the AI model (aaroh_model.pth) is trained to detect.

Rice Pests:
- Rice leaf roller
- Rice leaf caterpillar
- Rice leafhopper
- Asiatic rice borer
- Yellow rice borer
- Rice gall midge
- Paddy stem maggot
- Brown plant hopper
- White backed plant hopper
- Rice Stemfly

General/Broad-spectrum Pests:
- Army worm
- Black cutworm
- Large cutworm
- Yellow cutworm
- Aphids
- Red spider
- Longlegged spider mite
- Thrips
- Grain spreader thrips
- Grub
- Flea beetle
- Blister beetle
- Prodenia litura

Beet & Alfalfa Pests:
- Beet fly
- Beet weevil
- Alfalfa plant bug
- Therioaphis maculata Buckton
- Odontothrips loti

Vineyard/Citrus & Other Specialized Pests:
- Viteus vitifoliae (Grape phylloxera)
- Pseudococcus comstocki Kuwana (Comstock mealybug)
- Ampelophaga
- Lycorma delicatula (Spotted lanternfly)
- Cicadella viridis
- Erythroneura apicalis
- Phyllocnistis citrella Stainton (Citrus leafminer)
- Toxoptera aurantii (Black citrus aphid)
- Lawana imitata Melichar
- Salurnis marginella Guerr
- Deporaus marginatus Pascoe
- Limacodidae (Slug caterpillar moths)
